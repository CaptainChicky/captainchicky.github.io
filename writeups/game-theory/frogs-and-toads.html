Strategy

● If you can jump, always jump. Never slide when a jump is open.

● Never let two pieces going the same direction sit next to each other in the middle. That's a deadlock.

● When only slides are available, pick the one that sets up a jump on the next turn. Peeking 2 moves ahead always resolves it.
