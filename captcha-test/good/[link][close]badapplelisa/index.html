What is Life But a Bad Apple?
★★★★★
Even as time keeps slipping by, I remain shrounded in heavy languor. Even though I clutch my heart, it is drifting away from me. I never move of my own accord, just carried along through cracks in time. I know nothing of what’s around me. I know only of myself. Am I dreaming? Or seeing nothing at all? My words are useless, a waste to speak. To grieve is only tiring, better to live on feeling nothing.
Does a future exist in wasted time?
Do I even exist in a world like this?
